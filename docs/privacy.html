Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

"Why Houses Do That" operates a YouTube channel and an internal publishing tool. The tool uses the YouTube API Services solely to upload videos to, and read analytics for, the channel owned by the operator. It does not collect, store, or share personal information from any other user, and it is not offered to the public.

The tool accesses Google user data (channel video and analytics data) only for the operator's own channel, under the operator's own authorization. Data is used only to publish videos and generate internal performance summaries. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Use of this tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
